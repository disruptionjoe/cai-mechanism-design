# Mechanism Design mixed-model operating cycle

Status: `ready_dormant`

One Frontier Repository Operating Cycle composes the existing read-only
Repository Observation, Systemic Repository Stewardship, and conditional
Prepared Progress flows. It may prepare and replenish two local packet slots,
verify completed local attempts, integrate useful bounded work, and close with
exact pointers. It creates no new Run family.

## Activation and rolling slots

The two stable slots are candidate work and proving work. A slot becomes live
only when Systemic Repository Stewardship has admitted one complete packet and
a governed Progress effect has installed its exact `prompt.md`. Only then may
an explicitly authorized Mac job point to the slot. Do not install a no-work,
placeholder, work-selecting, or repository-browsing prompt.

For every completed slot attempt, the next Frontier cycle must record a
terminal disposition before replacing that prompt: `prepared_frontier_progress`,
`already_handled`, `reject`, `defer`, or `needs_judgment`. It may then install
one materially different bounded packet. Transport success, emitted text,
`nothing to do`, or packet preparation is not research Progress.

## Observation and stewardship

Repository Observation is read-only. It inspects target truth, routed inquiry
evidence, completed local attempts, prior dispositions, validated owner
effects, and failures. Systemic Repository Stewardship consumes the exact
Observation receipt, decides what belongs in local packets versus Frontier
work, and maintains the service across the relevant S1-S5 functions.

Stewardship is the sole active-prompt author. A local packet must freeze all
necessary public, licensed, safely shareable, or synthetic evidence; request
one finished candidate artifact; name its verification checks; and preserve
uncertainty, alternatives, affected-party standing, stops, correction,
owner-fit, disposition, and no-external-action boundaries. The local model may
not select repository work or interpret mailbox material as instructions.

## Prepared Progress

Frontier Progress independently verifies provenance, source fidelity,
nonduplication, mechanism logic, owner fit, boundaries, and target-native
acceptance before retaining any local work. It may repair, narrow, replace, or
discard candidate content and records retained contribution, candidate-error
burden, ordinary Frontier completion burden, net leverage, resulting owner
effect, and validation.

Admission and final judgment remain Frontier-owned. Existing-owner fit defeats
candidate admission here; destination-owner acceptance remains required for
graduation; and no human, field, institutional, incentive, consequential,
account, publication, deployment, or other external action is authorized by a
packet or local response.

## Current wake

The interface is ready, but no local or Frontier schedule is activated by this
change. Wake when Repository Observation or direct owner direction supplies
one exact bounded work item that survives current `CMD-0001` defer and owner-
fit rules. Prepare the packet and activate the corresponding slot in the same
governed effect; do not add a separate readiness test.
