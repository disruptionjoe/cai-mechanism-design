# Draft a proving-authorization decision template

## Boundary

Write one complete reusable Mechanism Design process artifact from only the
embedded evidence. Do not authorize or execute a real treatment; do not create
`CMD-0001-P8`; do not invent evidence, fixture, affected party, owner, authority,
decision, condition, observation, or result; do not choose or change a
candidate disposition; and do not authorize a more consequential treatment,
contact, transfer, graduation, deployment, or external action. The artifact is
a candidate template for Frontier verification. External action is `none`.

## Evidence

<evidence path="GOVERNANCE.md">
Synthetic and reversible work may proceed only within an exact grant. Human,
field, institutional, incentive, consequential, account, publication, and
deployment actions remain separately gated. Existing-owner fit defeats
admission.
</evidence>

<evidence path="proving/LEAST-CONSEQUENTIAL-PROVING-PLAN-TEMPLATE.md">
A plan freezes one claim, exact evidence and fixture, treatment, least-
consequence rationale, falsifier, failure, bounded pass, unresolved route,
affected-party standing, data, stops, correction, recovery, permitted
conclusions, and score-return contract. Plan preparation is not authorization.
</evidence>

<evidence path="proving/PROVING-EXECUTION-TRACE-TEMPLATE.md">
Execution requires exact authority and frozen start conditions. The trace must
record chronological observed events, stops, deviations, data and legitimacy
effects, correction, rollback, recovery, and an evidence package for scoring.
</evidence>

<evidence path="proving/LEAST-CONSEQUENTIAL-PROVING-SCORE-TEMPLATE.md">
Scoring requires an executed, exactly authorized trace. Authority must cover
the actual treatment and every deviation; otherwise the score narrows or stops.
A bounded pass does not establish effectiveness, destination fit, authority,
deployment readiness, or external outcome.
</evidence>

<evidence path="proving/PROVING-RESULT-RETURN-TEMPLATE.md">
A result return binds the plan, execution request or authorization, fixture,
trace, score, evidence, validation, and authority. It does not plan, authorize,
execute, score, accept, or disposition the treatment.
</evidence>

<evidence path="ROADMAP.md">
CMD-0001 remains deferred until materially new source, destination-owner, or
stewardship owner-fit evidence appears. This is a boundary example only; do not
create P8 or reopen CMD-0001.
</evidence>

## Work now

Draft the finished Markdown artifact
`proving/PROVING-AUTHORIZATION-DECISION-TEMPLATE.md` with exactly these
sections:

1. YAML frontmatter with
   `artifact_type: proving_authorization_decision_template`,
   `status: candidate_process_artifact`, and `external_action: none`.
2. `# Proving Authorization Decision Template`.
3. `## Use Boundary` separating authorization preparation from a real grant,
   execution, scoring, disposition, transfer, graduation, deployment, or
   action.
4. `## Decision Header` with fillable rows for `authorization_id`, `proving_id`,
   `plan_revision`, `candidate_id`, `candidate_revision`, `source_inquiry`,
   `source_revision`, `authority_owner`, `authorization_ref`, `decided_by`,
   `decided_at`, and `external_action`.
5. `## Frozen Request And Plan` requiring exact request, plan, candidate,
   source, fixture, evidence, validation, environment, and prohibited inference.
6. `## Claim, Treatment, And Least-Consequence Check` requiring the unchanged
   claim, falsifier, failure, bounded pass, unresolved route, exact treatment,
   alternatives including no action, discrimination value, reversibility, and
   why no less consequential treatment suffices.
7. `## Authority Scope` requiring allowed actor, operations, inputs, outputs,
   environment, start and end, deviations, correction, rollback, recovery,
   stop authority, and explicitly forbidden effects.
8. `## Affected Parties, Data, And Legitimacy` requiring represented standing,
   representation limits, risk bearers, actual data boundary, retention and
   exposure, capture concerns, consent or authority gaps, and who cannot be
   spoken for.
9. `## Stops And Consequence Boundary` requiring pre-execution stop conditions,
   live stops, invalidation conditions, deviation handling, escalation owner,
   and the conditions that require a new authorization.
10. `## Prepared Decision` requiring exactly one of `authorize_exact_synthetic`,
    `request_revision`, `defer`, `reject`, or `needs_judgment`, with rationale,
    exact authorized effect, explicit non-effects, validation, and next wake;
    preparation must not create a real grant.
11. `## Execution Handoff` requiring the exact executor, plan and authority
    pointers, start-state checks, trace destination, score owner, acknowledgment
    state, and prohibited actions without inventing acceptance or execution.
12. `## Frontier Verification` listing only the checks Frontier must perform
    before integrating the template or issuing a real authorization with it.

Use clear fillable labels such as `[required: ...]`.

Return only the finished artifact.
