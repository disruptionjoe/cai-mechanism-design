# Draft a least-consequential proving-score template

## Boundary

Write one complete reusable Mechanism Design process artifact from only the
embedded evidence. Do not execute a test, score a real candidate, create
`CMD-0001-P8`, invent evidence or results, change a disposition, authorize a
more consequential treatment, or claim repository changes. The artifact is a
candidate template for Frontier verification. External action is `none`.

## Evidence

<evidence path="proving/README.md">
A proving result returns the tested assumption, evidence grade, absorber or
failure, residual uncertainty, and permitted next disposition. A bounded pass
does not establish effectiveness, destination fit, authority, deployment
readiness, or external outcome.
</evidence>

<evidence path="proving/LEAST-CONSEQUENTIAL-PROVING-PLAN-TEMPLATE.md">
A plan freezes exact evidence, one claim, treatment and least-consequence
rationale, falsifier, observable failure, bounded pass, unresolved route,
affected-party standing, data boundary, stops, correction, recovery, permitted
conclusions, and a score-return contract. Plan preparation does not authorize
execution or scoring.
</evidence>

<evidence path="GOVERNANCE.md">
Synthetic and reversible work may proceed only within an exact grant. Human,
field, institutional, incentive, consequential, account, publication, and
deployment actions remain separately gated. Existing-owner fit defeats
admission.
</evidence>

<evidence path="proving/CMD-0001-P7-counterevidence-quality-score.md">
The P7 score separates the frozen baseline failure from the repaired rerun,
types counterevidence and residual uncertainty, limits the bounded conclusion,
and preserves defer. This is a score-shape example only; do not reuse its facts
or create another P7/P8 test.
</evidence>

<evidence path="dispositions/README.md">
Every result returns to kill, revise, defer, transfer, or propose graduation.
Preserve negative evidence and reasons. A clean artifact does not override
safety, legitimacy, source uncertainty, or owner consent.
</evidence>

## Work now

Draft the finished Markdown artifact
`proving/LEAST-CONSEQUENTIAL-PROVING-SCORE-TEMPLATE.md` with exactly these
sections:

1. YAML frontmatter with `artifact_type: least_consequential_proving_score_template`,
   `status: candidate_process_artifact`, and `external_action: none`.
2. `# Least-Consequential Proving-Score Template`.
3. `## Use Boundary` separating score preparation from execution,
   disposition, transfer, graduation, deployment, or action.
4. `## Score Header` with fillable rows for `proving_id`, `plan_revision`,
   `candidate_id`, `candidate_revision`, `source_inquiry`, `source_revision`,
   `treatment_type`, `result_owner`, `execution_authority_ref`, `scored_by`,
   `scored_at`, and `external_action`.
5. `## Frozen Plan And Evidence` requiring exact plan, request, response,
   evidence, fixture, and validation revisions plus prohibited inference.
6. `## Claim And Decision Conditions` requiring the exact claim, falsifier,
   observable failure, bounded pass, unresolved route, and prohibited
   conclusions copied from the frozen plan.
7. `## Observed Trace` requiring facts observed, transformations performed,
   stop events, missing evidence, and deviations from plan.
8. `## Evidence Grade And Counterevidence` requiring evidence type and grade,
   supporting evidence, counterevidence basis and posture, absorbers or failure,
   and whether the result discriminates.
9. `## Affected-Party, Data, And Legitimacy Check` requiring the actual standing,
   representation limits, risk, data boundary, capture concerns, and any
   authority or consent gap encountered.
10. `## Result` allowing exactly `bounded_pass`, `revise`, `kill`, `defer`, or
    `unresolved`, with one rationale and explicit disallowed interpretations.
11. `## Residual Uncertainty, Correction, And Recovery` requiring unresolved
    uncertainty, negative-evidence preservation, correction owner, rollback or
    recovery, and exact next wake.
12. `## Permitted Disposition Return` requiring the narrow owner disposition
    supported and explicitly excluding automatic transfer, graduation,
    deployment, or external action.
13. `## Frontier Verification` listing only the checks Frontier must perform
    before integrating the template or accepting a score made with it.

Use clear fillable labels such as `[required: ...]`.
Return only the finished artifact.
