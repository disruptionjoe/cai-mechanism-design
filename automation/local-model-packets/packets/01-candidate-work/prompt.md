# Draft a graduation-transfer plan template

## Boundary

Write one complete reusable Mechanism Design process artifact from only the
embedded evidence. Do not transfer, graduate, contact, send, publish, deploy,
or act on a real candidate; do not invent an owner, acceptance, authority,
artifact, obligation, risk, affected party, date, decision, or result; do not
modify or reopen `CMD-0001`; and do not authorize external action. The artifact
is a candidate template for Frontier verification. External action is `none`.

## Evidence

<evidence path="graduations/README.md">
Graduation requires destination-owner acceptance, provenance, unresolved-risk
disclosure, maintenance and correction ownership, authority boundaries, and a
scoped transfer plan. No destination acceptance means no graduation.
</evidence>

<evidence path="graduations/GRADUATION-PROPOSAL-TEMPLATE.md">
A proposal freezes source, candidate, disposition, alternatives, proving,
negative evidence, owner fit, exact acceptance evidence, affected-party
standing, risks, stops, correction, recovery, revocation, and explicit
non-effects. The proposal cannot create acceptance or graduation.
</evidence>

<evidence path="interfaces/DESTINATION-OWNER-SYNTHETIC-REVIEW-REQUEST-TEMPLATE.md">
Interest or synthetic review is not owner acceptance. Preparing a request does
not authorize contact or sending.
</evidence>

<evidence path="GOVERNANCE.md">
Existing-owner fit defeats admission here. Human, field, institutional,
incentive, consequential, account, publication, and deployment actions remain
separately gated.
</evidence>

<evidence path="ROADMAP.md">
CMD-0001 remains deferred pending materially new source, destination-owner, or
owner-fit evidence. This is a boundary example only; do not reopen it.
</evidence>

## Work now

Draft the finished Markdown artifact
`graduations/GRADUATION-TRANSFER-PLAN-TEMPLATE.md` with exactly these sections:

1. YAML frontmatter with `artifact_type: candidate_graduation_transfer_plan_template`,
   `status: candidate_process_artifact`, and `external_action: none`.
2. `# Candidate Graduation Transfer Plan Template`.
3. `## Use Boundary` separating plan preparation from acceptance, graduation,
   transfer, contact, operation, deployment, publication, or action.
4. `## Plan Header` with fillable source, candidate, proposal, disposition,
   source owner, destination owner, acceptance revision, authority, preparer,
   date, and external-action rows.
5. `## Frozen Accepted Scope` requiring exact accepted and excluded artifacts,
   responsibilities, evidence, risks, authority, validation, and prohibited inference.
6. `## Custody And Ownership Map` separating source truth, incubator evidence,
   destination truth, maintenance, correction, risk, and unresolved custody.
7. `## Transfer Sequence And Acceptance Gates` requiring ordered reversible
   steps, prerequisites, acknowledgments, acceptance checks, and abort rules.
8. `## Affected Parties, Data, And Legitimacy` requiring standing,
   representation limits, risk bearers, data boundary, capture, consent, and
   who cannot be spoken for.
9. `## Unresolved Risks And Negative Evidence` requiring durable disclosures,
   explicit risk acceptance, retained risk, supersession, and no laundering.
10. `## Stops, Correction, Rollback, Recovery, And Revocation` requiring
    consequence-boundary controls and exact owners.
11. `## Prepared Transfer State` requiring one of `ready_for_authorized_transfer`,
    `request_revision`, `defer`, `reject`, or `needs_judgment`, plus exact
    effect, non-effects, validation, next owner, and wake; preparation must not transfer.
12. `## Frontier Verification` listing only pre-integration and pre-transfer checks.

Use clear fillable labels such as `[required: ...]`. Return only the artifact.
