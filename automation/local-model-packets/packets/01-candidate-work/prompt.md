# Draft a candidate-admission decision template

## Boundary

Write one complete reusable Mechanism Design process artifact from only the
embedded evidence. Do not admit, reject, revise, reopen, or disposition a real
candidate; do not invent an inquiry, owner, source, affected party, fact, date,
or result; do not modify `CMD-0001`; and do not authorize contact, research,
testing, transfer, graduation, deployment, or external action. The artifact is
a candidate template for Frontier verification. External action is `none`.

## Evidence

<evidence path="GOVERNANCE.md">
A candidate needs a source inquiry, owner-fit rationale, theory of change,
alternatives, evidence route, affected-party and legitimacy analysis, stops,
correction path, and intended disposition. Existing-owner fit defeats admission
here. Synthetic and reversible work may proceed only within an exact grant;
human, field, institutional, incentive, consequential, account, publication,
and deployment actions are separately gated.
</evidence>

<evidence path="candidates/README.md">
Admission must preserve no action among the alternatives and keep candidate,
formal, simulation, and adversarial work evidence-typed rather than treating
them as automatic status changes.
</evidence>

<evidence path="dispositions/README.md">
Every candidate ends in kill, revise, defer, transfer, or propose graduation.
Negative evidence and reasons remain durable. A clean prototype does not
override safety, legitimacy, source uncertainty, or owner consent.
</evidence>

<evidence path="interfaces/DESTINATION-OWNER-SYNTHETIC-REVIEW-REQUEST-TEMPLATE.md">
Owner fit requires the proposed owner's native authority, the better-existing-
owner test, what remains with Mechanism Design, and the evidence still missing.
Interest or expertise alone does not prove fit.
</evidence>

<evidence path="ROADMAP.md">
CMD-0001 remains deferred until materially new source, destination-owner, or
stewardship owner-fit evidence appears. Do not create work merely to extend its
proving count. This is a boundary example only; do not reopen CMD-0001.
</evidence>

## Work now

Draft the finished Markdown artifact
`candidates/CANDIDATE-ADMISSION-DECISION-TEMPLATE.md` with exactly these
sections:

1. YAML frontmatter with `artifact_type: candidate_admission_decision_template`,
   `status: candidate_process_artifact`, and `external_action: none`.
2. `# Candidate Admission Decision Template`.
3. `## Use Boundary` separating decision preparation from admission,
   disposition, testing, contact, transfer, graduation, or action.
4. `## Decision Header` with fillable rows for `decision_id`, `source_inquiry`,
   `source_revision`, `candidate_label`, `decision_owner`, `requested_by`,
   `authorization_ref`, and `external_action`.
5. `## Inquiry And Problem Boundary` requiring the exact inquiry, affected
   condition, what is excluded, and prohibited inference.
6. `## Existing-Owner Fit` requiring owners considered, native authority,
   evidence for fit or non-fit, missing evidence, and the rule that a better
   existing owner defeats admission.
7. `## Candidate And Theory Of Change` requiring the candidate mechanism,
   inputs, causal steps, intended effects, assumptions, and failure modes.
8. `## Alternatives And No Action` requiring no action, at least one materially
   different alternative, owner routing, and kill/defer without forced parity.
9. `## Evidence And Proving Route` requiring exact source pointers, claim
   posture, counterevidence, least-consequential proving route, and conclusions
   that remain prohibited.
10. `## Affected Parties And Legitimacy` requiring standing, risk bearers,
    representation limits, capture risks, consent or authority gaps, and who
    cannot be spoken for.
11. `## Stops, Correction, And Recovery` requiring stop and kill conditions,
    correction owner, negative-evidence preservation, rollback, and next wake.
12. `## Prepared Decision` allowing exactly `admit_synthetic_candidate`,
    `route_to_existing_owner`, `request_revision`, `defer`, `reject`, or
    `needs_judgment`, with required rationale and unresolved risks.
13. `## Frontier Verification` listing only the checks Frontier must perform
    before integrating or using the template.

Use clear fillable labels such as `[required: ...]`.
Return only the finished artifact.
