# Draft a mechanism-alternative map template

## Boundary

Write one complete reusable Mechanism Design process artifact from only the
embedded evidence. Do not compare, admit, reject, revise, reopen, or disposition
a real candidate; do not invent an inquiry, owner, source, affected party,
mechanism, fact, date, authority, or result; do not modify `CMD-0001`; and do not
authorize contact, research, testing, transfer, graduation, deployment, or
external action. The artifact is a candidate template for Frontier
verification. External action is `none`.

## Evidence

<evidence path="GOVERNANCE.md">
A candidate needs a source inquiry, owner-fit rationale, theory of change,
alternatives, evidence route, affected-party and legitimacy analysis, stops,
correction path, and intended disposition. Existing-owner fit defeats admission
here. Synthetic and reversible work may proceed only within an exact grant;
human, field, institutional, incentive, consequential, account, publication,
and deployment actions are separately gated.
</evidence>

<evidence path="candidates/README.md">
Admission must preserve no action among the alternatives and keep candidate,
formal, simulation, and adversarial work evidence-typed rather than treating
them as automatic status changes.
</evidence>

<evidence path="candidates/CANDIDATE-ADMISSION-DECISION-TEMPLATE.md">
Admission preparation freezes inquiry and source revision, applies the better-
existing-owner test, makes causal steps and failure modes inspectable, preserves
no action and genuinely different alternatives without artificial parity, and
keeps affected-party, stop, correction, recovery, and authority limits explicit.
</evidence>

<evidence path="interfaces/DESTINATION-OWNER-SYNTHETIC-REVIEW-REQUEST-TEMPLATE.md">
Owner fit requires the proposed owner's native authority, the better-existing-
owner test, what remains with Mechanism Design, and the evidence still missing.
Interest or expertise alone does not prove fit, and preparing a request does not
authorize contact or sending.
</evidence>

<evidence path="dispositions/README.md">
Every candidate ends in kill, revise, defer, transfer, or propose graduation.
Negative evidence and reasons remain durable. A clean artifact does not override
safety, legitimacy, source uncertainty, or owner consent.
</evidence>

<evidence path="ROADMAP.md">
CMD-0001 remains deferred until materially new source, destination-owner, or
stewardship owner-fit evidence appears. Do not create work merely to extend its
proving count. This is a boundary example only; do not reopen CMD-0001.
</evidence>

## Work now

Draft the finished Markdown artifact
`candidates/MECHANISM-ALTERNATIVE-MAP-TEMPLATE.md` with exactly these sections:

1. YAML frontmatter with `artifact_type: mechanism_alternative_map_template`,
   `status: candidate_process_artifact`, and `external_action: none`.
2. `# Mechanism Alternative Map Template`.
3. `## Use Boundary` separating map preparation from candidate decision,
   disposition, testing, contact, transfer, graduation, or action.
4. `## Map Header` with fillable rows for `map_id`, `source_inquiry`,
   `source_revision`, `candidate_or_problem_ref`, `map_owner`,
   `authorization_ref`, and `external_action`.
5. `## Frozen Inquiry And Candidate Boundary` requiring the exact problem,
   candidate if any, exclusions, and prohibited inference.
6. `## Alternative Records` requiring no action plus one or more repeatable,
   materially different alternative records; each record must include mechanism,
   owner route, inputs, causal steps, assumptions, evidence posture, intended
   effect, failure modes, affected parties, stops, and correction.
7. `## Material Difference And Nonduplication` requiring the load-bearing
   distinctions, duplicate or absorber checks, and why variants were merged.
8. `## Existing-Owner Fit And Routing` requiring native authority, fit and
   non-fit evidence, missing evidence, and the better-existing-owner rule.
9. `## Evidence, Counterevidence, And Proving Needs` requiring exact pointers,
   claim posture, counterevidence basis and effect, discriminatory questions,
   and least-consequential proving needs without authorizing execution.
10. `## Affected Parties And Legitimacy` requiring standing, risk bearers,
    representation limits, capture risks, consent or authority gaps, and who
    cannot be spoken for.
11. `## Comparative Boundary` allowing incommensurable or dominated alternatives,
    refusing forced parity or fabricated ranking, and naming what cannot yet be
    compared.
12. `## Prepared Return` allowing exactly `retain_for_admission_review`,
    `route_to_existing_owner`, `request_revision`, `defer`, `reject`, or
    `needs_judgment`, with rationale, negative-evidence preservation, and next wake.
13. `## Frontier Verification` listing only the checks Frontier must perform
    before integrating or using the template.

Use clear fillable labels such as `[required: ...]` and a repeatable alternative
block rather than requiring an arbitrary fixed number.

Return only the finished artifact.
