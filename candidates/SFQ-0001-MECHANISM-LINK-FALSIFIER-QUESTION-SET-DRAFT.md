mechanism chain. Visibility-to-authority and authority-to-correction linkage,
affected-party standing, appeal and correction access, effectiveness, and new
capture surfaces remain unknown. Domain owners retain remedy meaning,
authority, and evaluation. `CMD-0001` remains deferred.
