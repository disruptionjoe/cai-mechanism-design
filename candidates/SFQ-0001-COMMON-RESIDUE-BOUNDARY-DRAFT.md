# SFQ-0001 Common-Residue Boundary Audit

## Use Boundary

This source-bounded audit tests only which frozen crosswalk roles explicitly
support the proposed common residue. It does not establish that every record
supplies every element, that visibility and correction are causally linked,
that an authority role is accountable to affected parties, or that any remedy
is effective or transferable. Domain owners retain remedy meaning, authority,
and evaluation. `CMD-0001` remains deferred.

  operational safety`.
