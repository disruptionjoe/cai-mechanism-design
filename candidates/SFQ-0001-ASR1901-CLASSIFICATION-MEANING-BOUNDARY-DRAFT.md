# SFQ-0001 ASR-19-01 Classification Meaning Boundary Draft

## Boundary

This draft applies the official NTSB meanings of `Closed - Acceptable Action`
and `Open - Acceptable Response` to the seven current overall classifications.
Frontier checked the definitions and all seven CAROL records on 2026-09-01,
but the artifact remains explicitly unverified research. A Board
classification describes the recipient-response posture; it does not by
itself independently establish implementation, outcome, effectiveness, cost,
enforceability, affected-party consequence, capture, transferability, remedy,
candidate action, contact, or external effect. `CMD-0001` remains deferred.
