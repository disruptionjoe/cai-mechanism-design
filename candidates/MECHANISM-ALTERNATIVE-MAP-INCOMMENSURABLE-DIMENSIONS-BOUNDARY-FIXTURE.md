## Alternative Records

### `SYNTH-ALT-INCOMMENSURABLE-NO-ACTION-001@r1`

No action preserves the invisible return. The owner route remains unresolved.
It adds no copied fields, synchronization, verification dependency, or false-
acceptance surface, but the correction can remain orphaned. Stop on any source,
authority, standing, custody, content, or risk revision. Recovery preserves the
frozen record and creates no route.

### `SYNTH-ALT-INCOMMENSURABLE-DIRECT-001@r1`

The direct route exposes a pointer-only return containing the immutable
pointer, checksum, correction owner, stop, and acknowledgment state as soon as
the synthetic return surface opens. It copies zero native-content fields and
adds no synchronization obligation. Its supported advantage is earlier
visibility. Owner route and acceptance remain unresolved. Failure modes are
false owner authority and acknowledgment treated as acceptance. Recovery
removes the proposed route and preserves the record.

### `SYNTH-ALT-INCOMMENSURABLE-VERIFIED-001@r1`

The verified route exposes the same pointer-only fields only after an
independent synthetic checksum reviewer has matched the immutable pointer and
checksum. It copies zero native-content fields and adds no synchronization
obligation. Its supported advantage is an independent checksum match before
visibility; its cost is that the return remains invisible until that review
occurs. The reviewer has no owner or acceptance authority. Failure modes are
review delay, unavailable review, false reviewer authority, and acknowledgment
treated as acceptance. Recovery removes the proposed route and preserves the
record.

## Material Difference And Nonduplication

The direct and verified routes are materially distinct because one exposes the
return before independent checksum review and one conditions visibility on
that review. Both preserve the same pointer-only content, unresolved owner
route, non-acceptance boundary, and recovery. Neither route is a duplicate or
dominated alternative under the supplied evidence.
